# 川文内置素材
# 格式：
#   @topic 标识 | English | 中文 | 图案字
#   p | 作文题 | 中文
#   c | 搭配 | 中文 | 例句（[方括号] 里是挖空答案，可以有几处）| 例句中文 | 近义说法（用 ; 隔开，可省略）
#   s | 句型 | 中文 | 例句 | 例句中文 | 说明（可省略）
#   l | 衔接 | 中文 | 例句 | 例句中文 | 近义说法
#   i | 论点（英文） | 中文
# sb / sth / one's 是占位，挖空时不算在答案里。

@topic general | General | 通用 | 通
s | It is not X but Y that ... | 不是 X 而是 Y …… | [It is not] wealth [but] health [that] determines how satisfied people feel in old age. | 决定人们晚年满意度的不是财富，而是健康。 | 强调句，适合纠正一个常见看法
s | Far from being X, ... | 远非 X，…… | [Far from being] a waste of time, video games can sharpen problem-solving skills. | 电子游戏远非浪费时间，它能锻炼解决问题的能力。
s | There is a growing consensus that ... | 越来越多的人认同…… | [There is a growing consensus that] climate change requires coordinated global action. | 越来越多的人认同，气候变化需要全球协同行动。
s | While it is true that X, Y | 诚然 X，但 Y | [While it is true that] online courses are convenient, they cannot fully replace face-to-face teaching. | 诚然网课很方便，但它无法完全取代面对面教学。 | 让步，先承认再转折
s | The more X, the more Y | 越 X，就越 Y | [The more] time children spend outdoors, [the more] confident they become. | 孩子在户外待的时间越多，就越自信。
s | Not only does X, but Y also ... | X 不仅……，Y 也…… | [Not only does] public transport reduce congestion, [but] it [also] cuts carbon emissions. | 公共交通不仅能缓解拥堵，还能减少碳排放。 | 倒装，not only 放句首时助动词提前
s | X plays a pivotal role in Y | X 在 Y 中起关键作用 | Teachers [play a pivotal role in] shaping students' values. | 教师在塑造学生价值观方面起着关键作用。
s | It would be naive to assume that ... | 以为……就太天真了 | [It would be naive to assume that] technology alone can solve social inequality. | 以为光靠技术就能解决社会不平等，未免太天真。
s | X is often cited as a prime example of Y | X 常被视为 Y 的典型例子 | Singapore [is often cited as a prime example of] efficient urban planning. | 新加坡常被视为高效城市规划的典型。
s | Given that X, it is hardly surprising that Y | 鉴于 X，Y 也就不足为奇了 | [Given that] housing prices have doubled, [it is hardly surprising that] young people delay marriage. | 鉴于房价翻了一倍，年轻人推迟结婚也就不足为奇。
s | Whether X or Y, ... | 无论 X 还是 Y，…… | [Whether] in cities [or] in the countryside, access to clean water is a basic right. | 无论在城市还是乡村，获得清洁用水都是一项基本权利。
s | X, which in turn leads to Y | X，进而导致 Y | Poor diets cause obesity, [which in turn leads to] a higher risk of heart disease. | 不良饮食导致肥胖，进而增加患心脏病的风险。 | 写因果链
s | Only when X can Y | 只有当 X 时，才能 Y | [Only when] the public is well informed [can] democracy function properly. | 只有公众充分知情，民主才能正常运转。 | 倒装
s | It is high time that sb did sth | 早该……了 | [It is high time that] governments [took] plastic pollution seriously. | 政府早该认真对待塑料污染了。 | that 从句用过去式
s | X is a double-edged sword | X 是一把双刃剑 | Social media [is a double-edged sword] for teenagers. | 社交媒体对青少年来说是一把双刃剑。
s | What matters most is not X but Y | 最重要的不是 X 而是 Y | [What matters most is not] how many hours students study [but] how effectively they use them. | 最重要的不是学生学多少小时，而是用得多有效。
s | Admittedly, X. Nevertheless, Y | 不可否认 X。尽管如此，Y | [Admittedly], electric cars are still expensive. [Nevertheless], their running costs are far lower. | 不可否认，电动车仍然很贵。尽管如此，它的使用成本低得多。
s | X, if anything, Y | 如果说有什么变化的话，X 反而 Y | Remote work has, [if anything], made people more productive. | 远程办公如果说有什么影响，反倒让人效率更高了。
s | There is no denying that ... | 不可否认…… | [There is no denying that] the internet has transformed the way we learn. | 不可否认，互联网改变了我们的学习方式。
s | Rather than X, Y should ... | 与其 X，Y 应该…… | [Rather than] banning smartphones, schools [should] teach students to use them wisely. | 与其禁止智能手机，学校更应教学生合理使用。
s | It goes without saying that ... | 不用说…… | [It goes without saying that] a healthy economy depends on a skilled workforce. | 不用说，健康的经济依赖于熟练的劳动力。
s | X comes at the expense of Y | X 以牺牲 Y 为代价 | Rapid growth often [comes at the expense of] the environment. | 快速增长往往以牺牲环境为代价。
s | So X that Y | 如此 X 以至于 Y | The benefits are [so] significant [that] they far outweigh the costs. | 好处如此之大，远远超过了成本。
s | Should X happen, Y | 万一 X，Y | [Should] the policy fail, millions of families would be affected. | 万一政策失败，数百万家庭将受到影响。 | 省略 if 的倒装条件句
l | By the same token | 同理 | Cities need more parks. [By the same token], they need safer cycling lanes. | 城市需要更多公园。同理，也需要更安全的自行车道。 | likewise; similarly
l | That said | 话虽如此 | Tourism brings money to local communities. [That said], it can also damage fragile ecosystems. | 旅游给当地社区带来收入。话虽如此，它也可能破坏脆弱的生态系统。 | having said that; even so
l | On the flip side | 另一方面（不利的一面） | Working from home saves commuting time. [On the flip side], it can blur the line between work and life. | 在家办公省了通勤时间。另一方面，它会模糊工作与生活的界限。 | on the other hand; conversely
l | In a similar vein | 同样地 | [In a similar vein], universities should reward teaching as much as research. | 同样地，大学应当像奖励科研一样奖励教学。 | along the same lines
l | It follows that | 由此可见 | Most crime is linked to poverty. [It follows that] reducing poverty will reduce crime. | 多数犯罪与贫困有关。由此可见，减少贫困就能减少犯罪。 | consequently; therefore
l | To put it another way | 换句话说 | [To put it another way], cheap goods often carry hidden environmental costs. | 换句话说，廉价商品往往暗藏环境成本。 | in other words
l | First and foremost | 首先，最重要的是 | [First and foremost], education must prepare students for real life. | 首先，教育必须让学生为现实生活做好准备。 | above all; most importantly
l | Be that as it may | 尽管如此 | Exams are stressful. [Be that as it may], they remain a fair way to assess students. | 考试压力很大。尽管如此，它仍是评估学生的公平方式。 | nonetheless; even so
l | In light of this | 鉴于此 | [In light of this], stricter regulations seem inevitable. | 鉴于此，更严格的监管似乎不可避免。 | given this; in view of this
l | Not to mention | 更不用说 | Fast food is high in salt, [not to mention] the amount of sugar it contains. | 快餐盐分很高，更不用说含糖量了。 | let alone; to say nothing of
l | All things considered | 综合考虑 | [All things considered], the advantages of city life outweigh its drawbacks. | 综合考虑，城市生活利大于弊。 | on balance; overall
l | As a case in point | 举个恰当的例子 | Renewable energy can be profitable. [As a case in point], Denmark exports wind power to its neighbours. | 可再生能源可以盈利。一个恰当的例子是，丹麦向邻国出口风电。 | for instance; to illustrate
l | Conversely | 反过来说 | Strong families support children. [Conversely], neglect can harm their development. | 健全的家庭支持孩子成长；反过来，疏于照顾则会损害孩子的发展。 | on the contrary; in contrast
l | In the long run | 从长远来看 | [In the long run], investing in education pays for itself. | 从长远来看，教育投入会自我回本。 | in the long term; ultimately
l | To a large extent | 在很大程度上 | Success depends [to a large extent] on hard work. | 成功在很大程度上取决于努力。 | largely; to a great degree
l | Equally important is | 同样重要的是 | [Equally important is] the need to train teachers properly. | 同样重要的是，要好好培训教师。 | just as important is
l | Hence | 因此 | Prices rose sharply; [hence] the drop in demand. | 价格大涨，因此需求下降。 | thus; therefore
l | Whereas | 而（对比） | Older people prefer newspapers, [whereas] young people get news online. | 老年人偏爱报纸，而年轻人在网上看新闻。 | while
l | Ultimately | 归根结底 | [Ultimately], it is up to individuals to look after their own health. | 归根结底，照顾自己的健康是个人的责任。 | in the end; at the end of the day
l | With this in mind | 考虑到这一点 | [With this in mind], schools should offer more practical courses. | 考虑到这一点，学校应开设更多实践课程。 | bearing this in mind
l | Contrary to popular belief | 与普遍看法相反 | [Contrary to popular belief], most immigrants pay more in taxes than they receive in benefits. | 与普遍看法相反，大多数移民缴纳的税多于领取的福利。 | despite common assumptions
l | In essence | 本质上 | [In essence], the debate is about freedom versus security. | 本质上，这场争论关乎自由与安全之间的取舍。 | essentially; fundamentally
l | Moreover | 此外 | Cycling keeps people fit. [Moreover], it costs almost nothing. | 骑车让人保持健康。此外，它几乎不花钱。 | furthermore; in addition
l | As a result | 结果 | Factories closed, and [as a result] unemployment soared. | 工厂关闭，结果失业率飙升。 | consequently; thus
